EduBoard PIC16
Trainer Kit

A streamlined, single-layer trainer board engineered to simplify learning and prototyping with the PIC16F877A microcontroller. Designed entirely in KiCad for educational purposes, eliminating complexity and focusing on modular jumper-based configurations for students and electronics enthusiasts.

Hardware Specifications

Display Peripherals

Features a 16x2 Character LCD and a 4-digit 7-segment display multiplexed via 74LS48 decoder for data visualization.

Power Subsystem

Onboard LM7805 voltage regulation from a robust DC barrel jack input, with optimized copper pouring for minimal ground noise.

Modular Routing

100% jumper-wire configuration via precision female headers, simplifying educational experiments and learning.
